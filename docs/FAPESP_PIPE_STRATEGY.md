## POSITION 2 — CONSULTOR CIENTÍFICO

### Official ***REMOVED*** terminology
`Consultor Científico` (listed under "Colaboradores" in the SAGe proposal form)

### Why this role — not Pesquisador Associado
- Consultores Científicos do NOT require formal institutional affiliation
- They do NOT need to be linked to a university at the time of the proposal
- They CAN be unpaid (zero cost to your budget — reduces scrutiny)
- Three ***REMOVED*** postdoctoral experiences make him highly credible to reviewers
- Philosophy of Mind / AI Epistemology is genuinely rare and differentiating

### His function in the proposal
Frame his role around three things reviewers care about:

**1. Epistemological framework of AI decision systems**
His contribution: defines the philosophical foundations of how the system
represents and reasons about organizational knowledge. This is not decoration —
***REMOVED*** reviewers increasingly care about interpretability and AI ethics.

**2. Research continuity guarantee**
Frame this as: *"The Consultor Científico provides methodological supervision
and ensures conceptual continuity of the research programme."*
This directly addresses the "solo founder risk" concern.

**3. Interdisciplinary credibility**
AI + Philosophy of Mind + Linguistics + Computer Science = a genuinely
interdisciplinary proposal. This is exactly what ***REMOVED*** favors.

### His Sumário Curricular should emphasize
- Three ***REMOVED***-funded postdoctoral projects (list each with process numbers)
- Academic publications in philosophy of mind or epistemology
- Any prior collaboration with AI/technology research
- Institutional affiliation (if any — even informal visiting researcher status helps)

### What NOT to say
Do not frame him as "backup in case something happens to the founder."
That signals fragility. Frame as:
> *"Senior methodological advisor responsible for ensuring the conceptual
> rigour and epistemological soundness of the AI architecture."*

## RESEARCH PROJECT FRAMING

### The title (suggested)
**"Sistema de Inteligência Decisional Baseado em Processamento de Sinais
Organizacionais: Uma Abordagem Interdisciplinar de IA, Linguística e
Epistemologia para Empresas de Base Tecnológica"**

English equivalent:
*"Decision Intelligence System Based on Organizational Signal Processing:
An Interdisciplinary AI, Linguistics and Epistemology Approach for
Technology-Based Companies"*

### The research question (mandatory for ***REMOVED***)
> *"Como sistemas de inteligência artificial podem transformar sinais
> organizacionais heterogêneos — incluindo feedbacks de clientes,
> interações de suporte e dados operacionais — em inteligência decisional
> estruturada e interpretável para lideranças executivas?"*

English:
*"How can artificial intelligence systems transform heterogeneous
organizational signals — including customer feedback, support interactions,
and operational data — into structured, interpretable decision intelligence
for executive leadership?"*

### Research hypothesis
> *"A integração de modelos de linguagem natural com taxonomias semânticas
> formais e frameworks epistemológicos permite construir sistemas de IA
> capazes de produzir recomendações decisoriais mais confiáveis, rastreáveis
> e interpretáveis do que abordagens puramente estatísticas."*

### The four research objectives (Fase 1 — 9 months)
1. Design and validate a formal ontological taxonomy for organizational signal classification
2. Build and evaluate a signal processing pipeline using large language models
3. Develop an interpretability framework for AI-generated decision outputs
4. Conduct proof-of-concept validation with 1-2 technology company design partners

### Scientific novelty argument (mandatory — ***REMOVED*** does not fund product dev)
> *"The novel contribution of this research lies in the systematic integration
> of three fields rarely combined in enterprise AI: computational linguistics
> (for signal taxonomy), epistemology of AI (for decision interpretability),
> and applied NLP (for signal processing). Existing commercial solutions
> treat organizational signal analysis as a purely statistical problem.
> This research proposes that accurate organizational decision intelligence
> requires formal linguistic and philosophical foundations — a hypothesis
> with no existing validated framework in the literature."*
